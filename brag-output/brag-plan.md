# MajiShift — four-minute demonstration

## Creative contract
Show a reservoir operator making a practical decision before a two-day pump interruption. The centerpiece is genuine application capture: inspect the weather contribution, compare schedules, edit starting storage, apply the edit, and export the result. 240 seconds, 1920×1080, silent by request. Captions are burned in; identical timed text is delivered as SRT and a voiceover-ready transcript.

## Planning rubric
1. Product: seven-day reservoir scenario planning with a reserve-first heuristic.
2. Strongest verified result: zero days below reserve versus five for the fixed schedule, with synthetic operating inputs.
3. Hook: when the pumps stop, the plan matters.
4. Actual UI: campus atlas, weather contribution, storage chart, daily schedule, editable assumptions and export.
5. Duration: four minutes, satisfying the competition's three-to-five-minute range.
6. Tone: polished, calm, specific; a practical operator story.
7. Audio: intentional silence; no music, sound effects or generated voice.
8. Share copy: MajiShift turns Conduit weather observations into an inspectable seven-day water-planning scenario.
9. Flow: inspect conditions → compare an outage → change storage → review the revised schedule → export.

Navy #0e3443, teal #087f84, paper #f4f7fa, ink #173342. Arial/Helvetica. Large explanatory text, real UI framed clearly, restrained pans, chapter labels and progress indicator. No fabricated live metrics.

## Evidence and boundaries
Weather comes from the official station 61 CSV, 29 August–4 September 2026. Daily reference ET is a climatic proxy, not measured dam evaporation. Radiation requires the FAO 0.408 MJ-to-mm conversion, corrected during demo QA. The corrected replay adds 35.6 m³ loss versus 4 mm/day; total pumping stays 7,875 m³ at the defaults. Do not claim pumping savings or a changed pumping schedule from this particular weather counterfactual. Operations and infrastructure remain unvalidated. All numerical captions must match evidence/calculations.json and captured UI.
